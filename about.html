my little story

22.
somewhere between figuring things out and enjoying the little things.

i like quiet mornings, long walks and books that stay in my head long after i've finished them.

i like learning things just because i'm curious. languages, psychology, little pieces of code — anything that makes the world feel a little bigger.

i'm still figuring out what i want my life to look like. and honestly, i think that's okay.
